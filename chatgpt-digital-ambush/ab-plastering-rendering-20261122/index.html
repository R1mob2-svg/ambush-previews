Interior finishes

Skimming & Plastering

Skimming is used to prepare walls and ceilings for a clean decorative finish. Depending on the condition of the surface, a project may involve preparation, repairs, a fresh plaster coat or finishing an existing wall. Before requesting a quote, it helps to explain the room sizes, surface condition and whether you need walls, ceilings or both. A discussion of the work can establish which preparation and finishing approach is suitable, without assuming every job needs the same treatment.

Outside the home

Exterior Rendering

Exterior rendering creates a protective decorative layer over suitable external wall surfaces. Different finishes and preparation requirements depend on the building fabric, existing coatings and condition of the walls. If you are considering rendering, describe the property, the area involved and any visible cracking or damage. Those details make it easier to discuss the finish you want and what work may be appropriate before agreeing a quotation. This concept does not assume that every rendering system or product is offered.

New partitions & interiors

Dry Lining & Boards

Dry lining uses plasterboard and associated finishing methods to form interior wall or ceiling surfaces. Work can involve boarding existing structures or forming partitions where the building layout permits. The materials, fixings and final finish depend on the space, the supporting structure and the intended use. When enquiring, identify whether the job concerns a new partition, existing wall, damaged boards or ceiling. Provide measurements and photographs if available so the scope can be discussed sensibly instead of being guessed.
