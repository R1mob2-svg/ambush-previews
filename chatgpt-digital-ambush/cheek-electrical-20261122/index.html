Electrical installation enquiries

Start with the right details.

Electrical work depends on the existing installation, how the space is used and the outcome required. These enquiry guides help a visitor prepare useful information before speaking directly with the business.

01 / Distribution

Consumer units and electrical updates

If the enquiry concerns an older board, additional protection or an installation change, note the current equipment, property type and anything that has recently changed. Clear photographs can help establish context, but suitability, testing, scope and any certification should be confirmed directly before a price or appointment is agreed.

02 / Alterations

Wiring, sockets and lighting enquiries

For a room alteration, new fitting or additional power point, explain where the work is needed, how the space will be used and whether decorating or other building work is planned. Cable routes, access and the condition of the existing installation may affect the available options, so the final method should be discussed with the business.

03 / Fault information

Tripping, loss of power and repair discussions

When something stops working or trips repeatedly, describe exactly what happened, which area is affected and whether anything was changed beforehand. Do not remove covers or attempt live electrical work. Isolate affected equipment only if it is safe to do so, then seek appropriate professional help. Availability and the correct diagnostic route must be confirmed directly.

Before you call

Useful questions to confirm directly.

What information should I have ready?
The property address, a short description of the work or problem, relevant photographs and details of any recent changes are useful starting points. The business can then confirm what else is needed.
Can a price be given from photographs?
Photographs may provide context, but some work can require an inspection or testing before scope and price can be established. Ask the business what is appropriate for your enquiry.
Which areas and appointment times are available?
The verified business address is in Shirley, Solihull. Current service coverage, availability and appointment arrangements should be confirmed directly using the published contact details.
What should I do if an electrical issue appears unsafe?
Do not attempt live electrical work. Stop using affected equipment where it is safe to do so and seek appropriate professional or emergency assistance based on the immediate risk.
