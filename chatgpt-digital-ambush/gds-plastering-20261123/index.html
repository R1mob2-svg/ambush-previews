Plastering & surface work

A better first impression, before the first call.

The presentation below uses service categories already attached to the existing GDS preview. Exact scope, property suitability, timing and availability should always be confirmed directly with the business.

01 · INTERNAL SURFACES

Skimming & plasterboarding

For enquiries about tired walls, new boards or surfaces being prepared for decoration, the useful starting point is the condition of the room rather than a generic package. Share clear photos, room dimensions and any known damp, cracking or previous repair history. GDS Plastering Limited can then confirm whether the enquiry fits its current work, what preparation may be relevant and what should be checked before agreeing a scope.

02 · EXTERNAL FINISH

Rendering enquiries

External finishes need a conversation about the existing substrate, exposed elevations, access and the result being considered. This page does not invent a particular render system or promise that every property is suitable. Instead, it gives homeowners and property managers a direct route to describe the building, send relevant information and ask GDS Plastering Limited what it can assess or quote for at the time.

03 · REPAIRS & PREPARATION

Patch repairs & making good

Smaller damaged areas can still need careful preparation, especially around previous fittings, chased services, cracks or adjoining finishes. When contacting GDS Plastering Limited, explain what caused the damage, whether the area is dry and stable, and what finish surrounds it. That context helps the business decide whether the repair is appropriate, whether wider preparation should be discussed and what information is needed before any price or visit is considered.
