Practical services

Start with the job in front of you.

Every enquiry begins with the property, the symptoms and the work you need considered. The examples below describe common conversation starters, without claiming accreditations or services that have not been independently verified.

Pipework & plumbing enquiries

For visible leaks, tired fittings, altered layouts or pipework that needs professional attention, share the location and a clear description of the problem. STM can then discuss the enquiry, ask the right practical questions and confirm whether the work fits current availability. Providing photographs, access details and any shut-off information at the start can help make that first conversation more useful.

Heating system enquiries

If a home is not warming evenly, controls are difficult to use or an existing heating setup needs attention, make contact with the symptoms and property details ready. This page does not claim any unverified registration or certification. It simply provides a clear route to ask about heating-related work, discuss what has been observed and confirm the appropriate next step directly with the business.

Planned improvements & property work

Plumbing and heating work often sits within a wider refurbishment, kitchen update or property improvement. If that is the case, outline the planned changes, the rooms involved and the timescale you are working towards. A useful initial enquiry should make clear what is existing, what you want to change and whether other trades are involved, so scope and scheduling can be discussed without guesswork.
