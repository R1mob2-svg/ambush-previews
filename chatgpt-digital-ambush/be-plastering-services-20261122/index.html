Walls & ceilings

Interior Plastering & Skimming

A fresh skim coat can help create a smooth surface for decorating, whether a room has been renovated, patched or fitted with new plasterboard. The right preparation depends on the existing wall, the soundness of old plaster and the finish required. When describing a project, note the room sizes, areas of cracking or damage and whether walls, ceilings or both need work. These details make it easier to discuss preparation, access and the scope of a quotation before work begins.

Ceiling refresh

Artex Overboarding & Skimming

B&E has shared a real public post about overboarding an artex ceiling and applying a skim finish. Overboarding can sometimes provide a new finishing surface where the existing coating is unsuitable, subject to assessing the ceiling's condition and structure. If you are planning a ceiling refresh, it helps to describe the existing texture, any damage or sagging, room size and access. Appropriate preparation and safe working methods must be determined for the actual property; this preview does not promise that one solution is right for every ceiling.

Preparation & finishing

Plasterboard & Wall Repairs

Where walls have been altered or damaged, plasterboard repairs and surface preparation may be needed before a smooth decorative finish is possible. This can involve assessing damaged areas, replacing unsuitable board, preparing joints and applying an appropriate finishing coat. Describe the affected rooms and share clear photos of holes, cracks, water damage or other defects when enquiring. The final method depends on inspection and the underlying cause of damage.
