What we do

Straightforward bathroom fitting and plumbing for homeowners and landlords — neat first-fix pipework, quality fixtures, and tidy site management throughout.

Pipework & Supplies

Supply line alterations & valves

Rerouting water feeds neatly, fitting isolation ball valves on every fixture, and upgrading low-pressure supplies for high-flow showers.

Before choosing an approach, consider the existing condition, access and the result you want to achieve. Clear photos and rough measurements can help make the initial discussion more useful, covering preparation, materials, any constraints and the practical next steps.

Plumbing Repairs

Leak fixes & joint repairs

Brazed copper connections, fixing stubborn pipe leaks beneath floors or in service ducts, and replacing worn waste traps.

Leaks, drainage problems and changes in water pressure can have different causes even when the symptoms look similar. A photograph of the fitting, any visible damage and a description of when the issue happens help clarify what needs checking and which repair options to discuss.

Complete Bathroom Fitting

Full bathroom renovations, suites & tiling

From stripping out old suites to wall boarding, tanking, precision tiling, fitting thermostatic showers, heated towel rails and vanity units — managed from start to finish without leaving you without facilities.

A finished room needs to work as well as it looks. Measurements, existing surfaces, preferred materials and details such as storage, lighting, ventilation and cleaning all deserve attention. Photos and a short list of priorities can help shape a practical layout and a clear scope.

Frequently asked questions

Do you manage the entire bathroom project from start to finish?
Yes. We handle strip-out, plumbing alterations, wall preparation, waterproof tanking, tiling, and sanitaryware installation so you don't have to juggle multiple contractors.
How long does a typical bathroom renovation take?
A standard family bathroom or ensuite refurbishment typically takes 6 to 9 working days from strip-out to final sealing, depending on tile choices and plumbing relocations.
Will I be left without a working toilet during renovation?
No. In homes with a single toilet, we reconnect temporary facilities at the end of each working day whenever possible so your household remains fully functional.
Do you carry out small plumbing repairs as well?
Yes. Replacing faulty stopcocks, sorting dripping mixer taps, clearing stubborn waste blockages, and installing outdoor garden taps are a regular part of our weekly schedule.
