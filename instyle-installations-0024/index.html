Questions

The things people ask before booking.

Do you measure before quoting?

Always on site. Guessing from photos is how fitted joinery ends up not fitting.

Can you work around irregular walls?

Yes — old houses are rarely square. That's exactly what site measuring is for.

Do you supply materials or use ours?

Either. We'll work with what you've chosen or supply timber to suit the job.

Will you tidy up daily?

Yes. Working area swept and tools away each day, particularly on fitted work inside a home.
