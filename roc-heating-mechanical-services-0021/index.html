Questions

The things people ask before booking.

How often does a boiler need servicing?

Once a year is the sensible default, and most warranties require it. It also catches the small failures before they become a cold house in winter.

Should I repair or replace my boiler?

Older boilers facing a large repair are often worth replacing, but not always. We'll give you the honest comparison rather than pushing a new install.

Can you help if my heating isn't working properly?

Yes — that's most of our work. Cold radiators, no hot water, pressure loss and noisy systems all get diagnosed before anything is priced.

Do you work on rented properties and letting agents?

Yes — we can deal with tenants directly where agreed, and provide written records of work for landlord compliance.
