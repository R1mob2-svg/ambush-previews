Frequently asked questions

Do you give a written quote before starting?
Yes. Every job, from replacing a ridge tile to a full re-roof, receives a clear written quote specifying materials and scope of work before we start.
How quickly can you attend to storm damage or active leaks?
We prioritise active water ingress and displaced tiles after high winds. Call us on 01675 462457 for swift emergency callouts to make your roof watertight.
Do you repair chimney stacks and lead flashing?
Yes. Degraded mortar joints and split lead flashing around chimneys are among the most common sources of ceiling leaks. We repoint stacks, replace flashing with proper code lead, and fit cowls.
Do you handle insurance repair work?
Yes, we provide itemised quotes and photographic evidence for insurance claims resulting from storm, tree, or structural roof damage.
