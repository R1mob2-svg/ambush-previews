Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers Before We Start

Everything you need to know about working with A J CANE PAINTING & DECORATING SPECIALISTS Ltd.

Do you handle planning permission and building regulations?

Yes. We handle all architectural plans, structural calculations, and building control applications through to full sign-off.

Are your quotes fixed in price before work begins?

Yes. Every quotation is fully itemised with a fixed-price schedule of works. You know exactly what is included from day one.

What areas across the West Midlands do you cover?

We are based on Mounts Road in West Midlands (WS10) and manage projects across West Midlands, Walsall, Cannock, and the wider West Midlands.

Can we live in our home during the main works for a painting & decorating services project?

In most cases, yes. We erect temporary dust seals, maintain temporary cooking facilities where possible, and clean the site thoroughly at the end of each working day.
