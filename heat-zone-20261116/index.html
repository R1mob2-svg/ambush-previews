Frequently asked questions

Do you give a price before starting work?
Yes. For any repair, boiler replacement, or pipework modification, you will receive a transparent price upfront. If an unexpected issue is uncovered, we stop and talk to you before proceeding.
My boiler loses pressure repeatedly — what causes that?
Repeated pressure drops usually indicate a minor pinhole leak on radiator pipework, a faulty pressure relief valve, or a depleted expansion vessel. We pressure-test the circuit to find the root defect.
Can you powerflush old radiators?
Yes. Radiators that are cold at the bottom usually contain magnetite sludge. A professional powerflush with cleansing chemicals clears the sludge and restores full heat output to every room.
How quickly can you attend for heating emergencies?
We prioritise complete loss of heat or hot water during cold months. Call us directly on 01922 453123 for fast local attendance in Aldridge and Walsall.
