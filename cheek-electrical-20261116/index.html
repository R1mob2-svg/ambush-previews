Questions we get asked

Do you give a price before starting?
Yes. For anything beyond a small repair you'll get the price up front, and if a job turns out bigger than expected we stop and talk to you before carrying on.
Can you work around us being at work?
Usually, yes. Access arrangements and key handover are standard for a lot of the domestic work — just say what suits you when you get in touch.
My fuse board keeps tripping — is it urgent?
Not always, but it shouldn't be left. Constant tripping usually points at a fault or an old board without proper protection, and both get worse rather than better. Worth a look sooner rather than later.
Do you do small jobs?
Yes — extra sockets, a new light, an outside socket, sorting a dead circuit. Small jobs are a normal part of the week.
Do you cover commercial premises?
Small commercial and light industrial work is fine. For anything larger, ring and we'll be straight with you about whether it's the right fit.
